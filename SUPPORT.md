# Get help

Start with [getting started](docs/getting-started.md), [data and recovery](docs/data-and-recovery.md), or [platform limits](docs/platform-and-limits.md).

For questions and bugs, use [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/asMagicBrain/App/issues), or email [AncoraSIR@gmail.com](mailto:AncoraSIR@gmail.com) with **asMagicBrain support** in the subject. Support depends on maintainer availability.

## Report a bug

Include the app version/build or source tag, operating system and architecture, and whether you downloaded or built the app. On Linux, include the desktop and X11/Wayland session.

Describe the steps, expected result, actual result, and displayed error. Add a small synthetic example if needed.

## Protect your data

Do not upload a whole workspace, profile, private repository, credential, or recovery directory. Remove personal paths, remote URLs, account details, commit identities, and document content from logs and screenshots.

For Save, draft, ownership, or update problems, close the app normally and preserve the original data. Investigate an isolated copy. Do not delete journals, ownership records, preserved documentation editions, or Git metadata to force a retry.

Report vulnerabilities through [Security](SECURITY.md) and conduct concerns through the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). Support cannot guarantee data recovery, account eligibility, release dates, or compatibility with every system.
