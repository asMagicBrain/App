# Code of Conduct

Treat users, contributors, and maintainers with respect. Discuss ideas on their merits, give constructive feedback, and respect differences in identity, experience, and communication.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory insults, intimidation, sexual harassment, publishing private information without permission, and repeated disruption are unacceptable. This policy covers project spaces and anyone explicitly representing the project elsewhere.

## Report a concern

Email [AncoraSIR@gmail.com](mailto:AncoraSIR@gmail.com) with **asMagicBrain conduct** in the subject. Include relevant context and only the personal information needed to understand it. Do not post sensitive details publicly. If the concern involves that recipient, first request an alternate private contact without sending the report details.

## How maintainers respond

Maintainers may warn participants, remove content, close discussions, limit participation, or exclude someone according to the evidence, severity, and pattern of behavior. They should apply this policy consistently, disclose conflicts of interest, and explain decisions when doing so is safe.

Reports should be shared only with people needed to resolve them. Absolute confidentiality and a response or resolution time cannot be promised.
